The Home
A different side of Kitsilano living: two levels, a garden you can actually live in, and room for more than the everyday.

With 1,149 sq ft, two bedrooms, two full bathrooms, a den and a windowed office, this patio home gives you the space most West Side condos can't. It feels like a little townhouse, with a private garden and your own gate to the lane.

Nine-foot ceilings and an open main floor connect the living, dining and kitchen areas. A floor-to-ceiling bookcase and tiled fireplace anchor the living room. Upstairs, the bedrooms and workspaces sit away from the everyday bustle.

Morning

Coffee in the garden

Step out to a private, fenced patio with a pergola, mature planting and cedar fencing. It's a real outdoor room for morning coffee, long dinners and quiet afternoons.

Your own gate opens straight to the lane, which makes a bike ride or a dog walk easy. Dogs are welcome here, with strata restrictions.

Workday

An office with a window, and a den besides

Working from home doesn't mean working from the kitchen table. Upstairs you'll find a windowed office/studio, a dedicated desk area and a den, so two people can each have their own space.

In-suite laundry and good storage round out the practical side.

Evening

A kitchen that keeps the cook in the conversation

White shaker cabinetry, a gas range, stainless-steel appliances and under-cabinet lighting, with a peninsula that looks out over the dining and living areas. You can make dinner and still keep an eye on the little ones while they play.

A full bathroom on the main floor means no running upstairs mid-meal, and on a summer night the party spills out through the patio doors.

Night

Kids asleep upstairs, grown-ups still up

With the bedrooms on their own floor, the kids can go down while the adults keep entertaining below. The generous primary bedroom has corner windows looking into the trees, and the second bedroom and a second full bathroom are on the same level.

Just down the hall, the building has a large, skylit playroom for rainy days. The fully rainscreened building also has a residents' workshop and a shared rooftop deck with city and North Shore mountain views.

Ownership

Prepaid leasehold, explained

This home is a prepaid strata leasehold, with a lease running to 2071. That's why you can own 1,149 sq ft with a private garden in Kitsilano at this price: in my experience, homes like this trade at more than 20% below similar freehold. You can put the difference to work elsewhere, or simply get a lot more home for the same money.

  • The lease is prepaid, so there are no monthly land-lease payments.
  • Conventional financing is available through select lenders.
  • You pay strata fees and property taxes, as with any condo.

With about 45 years left on the lease, I don't expect expiry to have a real effect on values for at least another 15 years. I'm happy to walk you through the lease summary and the lenders who work with it.
